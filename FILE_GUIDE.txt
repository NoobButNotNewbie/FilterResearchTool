FILTERTOOL - HUONG DAN CAC FILE
================================

File nay tom tat chuc nang va tinh chat thong tin trong cac file cua du an.
Du lieu trong data/ va output/ la du lieu runtime; cac file co the thay doi
sau khi chay pipeline.

1. FILE O THU MUC GOC
---------------------

README.md
  Huong dan muc dich, cai dat, lenh CLI va cac file dau ra. Thong tin mang
  tinh huong dan su dung, khong phai du lieu paper.

  Tao hai bang cheo tu manual codes: Optimization Technique x Attack Method va
  Optimization Technique x Objective; dem k/N theo hang, cot va tong. O thua
  chi la candidate sparse cell, khong phai research gap da xac nhan.
  Chua tham so va tu khoa do nguoi dung chon; khong chua ket qua paper.

  Mo rong references/cited-by tu paper da duoc nguoi Include; luu seed, huong,
  paper moi/da co va loi API de truy vet snowballing.
  cau hinh build. La thong tin cau truc/build cua phan mem.

  Tao auto taxonomy suggestions chi cho paper da human-include; khong tu quyet
  dinh inclusion va khong ghi de manual codes.
  Tai lieu nay: mo ta vai tro va loai thong tin cua cac file trong du an.

  Nap task, attack-stage, optimization-technique va optimization-objective
  dimensions; tao nhan goi y, bao gom none_detected/other_improvement.
---------------------------

  Yeu cau it nhat mot LLM/agent term va mot offensive-security term. Khong dat
  rule hoac khop noise exclusion thi dua vao human review, khong auto-exclude.
  Danh dau package filtertool va thong tin package co ban.

  Doi chieu metadata voi API de tham khao; verification khong thay the human
  screening va khong tu quyet dinh inclusion.

 screening.py
  Xuat/import screening workbook CSV/XLSX; validate Include/Exclude/Unsure,
  stage, reason code va luu audit event theo title/abstract hoac full text.

 coding.py
  Import manual taxonomy codes cho paper human-included va tinh exact agreement,
  Cohen's kappa giua suggestions auto va codes manual.

 prisma.py
  Tao PRISMA-style flow counts tu paper records, search log va human audit.
  Export counts theo source, stage va exclusion reason ra CSV/JSON.

 provenance.py
  Tao config hash da redact credential va ghi append-only search log.

Raw search records
  output/raw_search_results.jsonl luu tung Paper record da chuan hoa tu adapter,
  kem source/query/run id truoc dedup va filtering. Khong phai raw HTTP response.
  hay du lieu nghien cuu.

  Danh sach day du paper o dang JSON. Moi ban ghi co metadata, status, human
  decision, screening audit, auto/manual coding va citation provenance.
  cac o thua co the goi y khoang trong nghien cuu. Xu ly du lieu phan tich.

 output/screening_sheet.xlsx / fulltext_screening_sheet.xlsx
  Workbook cho human screening; co cot decision/reason code/reviewer/date.

 output/classified_papers.xlsx
  Coding workbook chi cho paper human-included; cot auto suggestions tach khoi
  manual codes, baseline_compared va measured.
  Mo rong tap paper qua references va cited-by. Xu ly metadata paper lay tu
  cac API trich dan.
  Workbook tu manual codes: hai cross-tab, candidate sparse cells va sheet
  Validation Search cho researcher ghi cach kiem tra cell thua.

 output/search_log.csv / run_manifest.json
  Protocol/config hash, source/query, cache use, result counts, errors, tool/model
  version va trang thai valid/invalid cua moi lan chay.

output/raw_search_results.jsonl
  Source records truoc cac buoc dedup/rule/semantic; dung de doi chieu thu thap.

 output/prisma_counts.csv / prisma_counts.json
  So lieu flow theo nguon, duplicate, screening decision, exclusion reason va
  snowballing seed/direction; khong tu ket luan PRISMA da hoan tat.
  Khai bao lenh command-line: chay pipeline, xem trang thai, export, reset va
  import CSV. La lop giao tiep, khong phai noi luu du lieu chinh.

config.py
  Doc va truy cap cau hinh YAML. Xu ly tham so cau hinh.

dedup.py
  Phat hien paper trung lap dua tren DOI va tieu de. Xu ly metadata dinh danh.

export.py
  Xuat danh sach paper sang JSON va workbook Excel cho phan loai/phan tich;
  dinh dang cot, lien ket, bang cheo va sheet Candidate Gaps.

models.py
  Dinh nghia Paper, PaperStatus, Source va ban ghi quyet dinh sang loc. Mo ta
  cau truc metadata va lich su xu ly cua mot paper.

normalize.py
  Chuan hoa DOI, tieu de va ten tac gia de tim kiem/doi chieu.

pipeline.py
  Dieu phoi cac buoc tim kiem, chuan hoa, loc, kiem chung, phan loai, phan tich
  va xuat. Ket hop du lieu tu cac module.

storage.py
  Luu paper va cache API trong SQLite; cung cap tra cuu theo ID, DOI, tieu de
  va trang thai. Payload paper la JSON ben trong database.

verify.py
  Doi chieu metadata paper voi cac nguon API. Tao ket qua khop/lechs/loi theo
  tung nguon de lam dau vet kiem chung.

classify/__init__.py
  Danh dau package con phan loai.

classify/classifier.py
  Ap dung taxonomy len paper va cap nhat nhan phan loai.

classify/taxonomy.py
  Nap cac nhom taxonomy va tu khoa; phan loai van ban thanh nhieu nhan.

filter/__init__.py
  Danh dau package con loc paper.

filter/rule_filter.py
  Loc theo tu khoa bao gom/loai tru; tao ket qua va ly do sang loc.

filter/semantic_filter.py
  Cham diem tuong dong ngu nghia bang sentence-transformers va gan nhan HIGH,
  REVIEW hoac LOW.

search/__init__.py
  Danh dau package con tim kiem.

search/base.py
  Lop nen cho adapter API, request HTTP va cach doc response.

search/arxiv.py
  Tim paper tren arXiv va chuyen response thanh metadata Paper.

search/crossref.py
  Tim paper qua Crossref va chuyen response thanh metadata Paper.

search/openalex.py
  Tim paper qua OpenAlex va chuyen response thanh metadata Paper.

search/semantic_scholar.py
  Tim/tra cuu paper, references va cited-by qua Semantic Scholar.

3. DU LIEU RUNTIME
------------------

data/papers.sqlite3
  Database SQLite chinh: metadata va payload JSON cua paper, trang thai xu ly,
  cung cache response API. Day la nguon du lieu co the tiep tuc chay pipeline.

data/cache/
  Thu muc cache response API theo file JSON, co the con lai tu cac phien ban cu.
  Noi dung la response ky thuat, khong phai bao cao tong hop.

output/all_papers.json
  Danh sach day du paper o dang JSON. Moi ban ghi co metadata, nguon, trang
  thai, diem/ket qua loc, kiem chung, nhan phan loai va lich su xu ly.

output/auto_suggestions.xlsx
  Taxonomy suggestions cho candidate; khong doi status review va khong phai
  final inclusion.

output/classified_papers.xlsx
  Coding workbook cho paper human-included; auto suggestions tach khoi manual codes.

output/crosstab.xlsx
  Workbook phan tich: sheet CrossTab la bang cheo va danh dau o thua; sheet
  Candidate Gaps liet ke method, technique, k/N, ty le va ID paper lien quan.

4. FILE DAU RA CU
-----------------

Neu cac file sau van con trong output/, do la ket qua tu phien ban export cu.
Pipeline moi khong cap nhat/tai tao chung:

output/all_papers.xlsx
  Ban Excel cu cua toan bo danh sach paper; noi dung trung lap voi
  output/all_papers.json.

output/filtered_papers.xlsx
  Ban Excel cu cua danh sach paper qua loc; la mot tap con theo trang thai.

output/candidate_gaps.json
  Ban JSON cu cua danh sach gap; thong tin nay hien duoc dat trong sheet
  Candidate Gaps cua output/crosstab.xlsx.
